# examples/locomotion

## Intent
RL locomotion stack for the Unitree Go2 in Genesis: a scripted-trot demo whose control and reward math run as compiled EvoX Compiler (`evoxc`) graphs, an independent TensorNEAT neuroevolution training entry for walking on the same compiled-graph layer, plus an rsl-rl PPO training/eval stack and playback of pre-trained backflip policies.
The TensorNEAT stack (`go2_neat_kernels.py`, `go2_neat_env.py`, `go2_device_rollout.py`, `go2_neat_train.py`) sits on the task-agnostic layer in `../neuroevolution/` and is reproducible from zero with the pinned baseline, commands and measured numbers below; it runs either host-staged (`residency="numpy"`, the reference) or zero-copy device-resident (`residency="device"`, IREE only).

## API Surface

### Scripted trot on EvoX Compiler graphs
- `try1.py` → the demo. Default = viewer until the window is closed (under pytest: no viewer, 5 steps); `--max-steps N` bounds the viewer run; `--headless` runs 250 steps (5 s), keeps per-step rewards, writes `go2_reward.png` next to the script and prints cumulative total + final pose.
  `--evox-backend` / `--evox-device` select the compiled-graph backend/device (default `numpy` / `cpu:0`); the resolved backend, device and compile time are printed.
- `go2_evox_kernels.py` → the two `evoxc` graphs (pure evoxc ops, no torch, no genesis) plus build/run helpers; both graphs are compiled ONCE before the step loop and reused every step.
  `GAIT` runtime inputs: `step` (0-d int32), `previous_action` (float32, 12) → outputs `targets`, `action`, `action_change` (float32, 12 each).
  `GAIT` static specializations: `standing_flat` (12), `phases` (4, `[0, pi, pi, 0]` per leg), `dt`, `inv_action_scale` (= 1/0.25).
  `REWARD` runtime inputs: `base_pos`(3), `base_quat`(4), `base_lin_vel_world`(3), `base_ang_vel_world`(3), `dof_pos`(12), `action_change`(12), all float32 → dict of the six Go2 Walking terms (`tracking_lin_vel`, `tracking_ang_vel`, `lin_vel_z`, `base_height`, `action_rate`, `similar_to_default`) plus `total`.
  `REWARD` static specializations: `standing_flat` (12), `dt`.
  Helpers: `DT`, `ACTION_SCALE`, `STANDING` (4×3) / `STANDING_FLAT` (12), `REWARD_TERM_NAMES`, `resolve_backend_device(backend, device)`, `build_gait()`, `build_reward()`, `run_gait(executable, step, previous_action)`, `run_reward(executable, ...)`; private `_constant`, `_to_device_array`, `_to_numpy` are the operand-staging helpers the neat modules reuse.
- `go2_legacy_reference.py` → the pre-migration math as importable pure functions and the parity oracle: `gait_step(step, previous_action, ...)` (numpy float64) and `walking_reward_terms(...)` (torch float32 state terms, numpy float64 action rate). No genesis import; quaternion helpers are transcribed from `genesis.utils.geom`.

### TensorNEAT Go2 walking graphs (`go2_neat_kernels.py`)
- Pure-`evoxc` module (no `genesis`, no simulation): the two Go2 task graphs (`build_obs`/`run_obs`, `build_advance`/`run_advance`) plus the `go2_env` task constants.
- It imports the shared task math from `go2_evox_kernels.py` — `DT`, `STANDING_FLAT`, `_constant`, `_to_device_array`, `_to_numpy`, `resolve_backend_device` — and the task-agnostic half from `../neuroevolution`: `build_policy`/`run_policy` (`policy_graphs`), `build_fitness`/`run_fitness`/`build_tell`/`run_tell`/`ExecutableCache` (`evolution_graphs`), `EPISODES_PER_POLICY`/`Perturbation` (`seeding`) and the staging helpers `_attach`/`_device_name`/`_outputs`/`_positive_int`/`_rows_of`/`_staged` (`evox_runtime`); no graph or helper logic is duplicated here.
- `_ensure_shared_layer_path()` is the stack's only `sys.path` shim: it runs at import time and puts `<repo>/examples/neuroevolution` on `sys.path`, so every other module of this stack imports `go2_neat_kernels` first and then the shared layer by bare module name.
- `build_policy(algo, config, pop_arrays, rows, *, backend, device)` is a one-line adapter over the shared policy graph that binds `num_inputs=NUM_OBS` / `num_outputs=NUM_ACTIONS` (and `episodes=EPISODES_PER_POLICY`); `run_policy`, `build_fitness`, `run_fitness`, `build_tell`, `run_tell` and `ExecutableCache` are re-exported unchanged.
- Constants: `NUM_OBS=45`, `NUM_ACTIONS=12`, `CLIP_ACTIONS=100.0`, `TERMINATION_LIMIT_DEG=10.0`, `OBS_SCALES`, `REWARD_SCALES`, `TRACKING_SIGMA=0.25`, `BASE_HEIGHT_TARGET=0.3`, `DEFAULT_COMMANDS`; `EPISODES_PER_POLICY=3` and `Perturbation` are re-exported from `../neuroevolution/seeding.py`.
  `OBS_SCALES` / `REWARD_SCALES` / `DEFAULT_COMMANDS` / `TRACKING_SIGMA` / `BASE_HEIGHT_TARGET` are `go2_env`'s task config (`obs_cfg` / `reward_cfg`), which differs from `try1.py`'s Go2 Walking terms, so they are restated here rather than imported.
- `DEFAULT_COMMANDS = [0.5, 0, 0]` is exact (not an approximation): `go2_train.get_cfgs()` uses singleton ranges, so `go2_env`'s command resampling is a no-op.
- `Perturbation(base_pos, base_rpy, joint_pos)` — the shared frozen reset-amplitude dataclass with `is_zero`; zero = the original fixed reset. The RNG-side `perturbed_init_qpos` belongs to the env module, not here.
- `build_obs(rows, *, standing_flat, commands, backend, device)` / `run_obs(exe, *, base_ang_vel_world, base_quat, dof_pos, dof_vel, actions, commands)` → `(rows,45)`, same layout/scaling as `go2_env._update_observation`.
- `build_advance(rows, *, max_episode_length, ...)` / `run_advance(...)` → `{"obs","reward","returns","alive","done","episode_length"}`: clips raw actions to ±100, the six dt-scaled `go2_env` reward terms, `alive`/`done`/`returns` masking, int32 episode counter, arctan2-free roll/pitch termination.
- `build_fitness`/`run_fitness`, `build_tell`/`run_tell` and `ExecutableCache` are the shared objects of `../neuroevolution/evolution_graphs.py`, re-exported here: the fitness is the per-policy episode mean (`(NP,)`, unscored episode counted as 0), `tell()` returns `{"randkey","generation","pop","species"}` with the species pytree captured during the trace, and the cache is shape-keyed so generation N+1 reuses generation N's executables.

### TensorNEAT Go2 walking trainer (`go2_neat_env.py` + `go2_neat_train.py`)
- Two modules layered on `go2_neat_kernels.py`: `go2_neat_env.py` is the batched, seeded evaluation env (no CLI); `go2_neat_train.py` is the CLI entry that drives NEAT generations through it.
- `go2_neat_env.py` builds ONE `gs.Scene` with `n_envs = rows = EPISODES_PER_POLICY * pop_size` (policy `p` owns rows `3p, 3p+1, 3p+2`), with the same plane+Go2 morphs, `SimOptions(dt=0.02, substeps=2)` and `RigidOptions(enable_self_collision=False, max_collision_pairs=20, tolerance=1e-5)` as `go2_env.py`.
- `CFG` is a literal copy of the authoritative `go2_train.get_cfgs()` numbers (joint names, default dof angles, kp/kd, base init pos/quat, `clip_actions`, `action_scale`, termination thresholds, `tracking_sigma`, `base_height_target`, `episode_length_s`, command ranges), because `go2_train` cannot even be imported without `rsl-rl-lib`.
- Public API of `go2_neat_env.py`: `generation_seed_table(master_seed, num_generations, episodes=EPISODES_PER_POLICY) -> (G,3) int64`, `evolution_seed(master_seed) -> int`, `Perturbation` and `EPISODES_PER_POLICY` are re-exported from `../neuroevolution/seeding.py`; `perturbation_from_seed(seed, perturbation, num_dof=NUM_ACTIONS)` is a one-line adapter over `seeding.perturbation_from_seed` (whose `num_dof` is mandatory) that keeps the Go2 default; `perturbed_init_qpos(init_qpos, seeds, perturbation) -> (3,19) float32` is the task's own qpos mapping; `class Go2NeatEnv(BatchedEpisodeEnv, pop_size, master_seed, *, perturbation=Perturbation(), horizon=None, episode_length_s=None, backend="numpy", device=None, show_viewer=False, seed_table=None, stop_when_done=True, residency="numpy")`.
- `residency` selects where the values that cross a graph boundary live: `"numpy"` (default, the reference: `read_state` -> host arrays -> `run_*`) or `"device"` (opt-in zero-copy IREE loop in `go2_device_rollout.py`); a `"device"` request on a cpu-kind `evoxc` device raises `ValueError` in `__init__`, before any scene work.
- `env.residency` is the resolved mode; both modes return the identical result mapping and both end `evaluate` with `validate_evaluation`.
- `Go2NeatEnv` subclasses the `env_base.BatchedEpisodeEnv` protocol and sets `episodes_per_policy = EPISODES_PER_POLICY`, which together with `.rows` / `.max_episode_length` / `.cache` / `.evaluate` / `.close` is the whole contract a `trainer.NeuroevolutionTrainer` drives; `evaluate` runs `env_base.validate_evaluation` on its result before returning it.
- The pipeline stub of `_build_policy_trace()` is the shared `trainer.TraceOnlyProblem(num_inputs=NUM_OBS, num_outputs=NUM_ACTIONS)` and the payload is read with `trainer.pop_arrays_of(state)`.
- `Go2NeatEnv.evaluate(pop_arrays, generation)` returns `{"seeds" (3,), "episode_returns" (NP,3), "returns" (rows,), "fitness" (NP,) float32, "steps", "env_steps", "wall_time_s", "steps_per_s", "env_steps_per_s", "cache_hits", "cache_misses", "end_reasons", "init_qpos"}`; `steps` counts simulated steps and `env_steps = steps * rows`.
- `Go2NeatEnv` also exposes `.rows`, `.episodes_per_policy`, `.max_episode_length`, `.cache`, `.executables()` (the four env-side graph executables), `.read_state()`, `._write_targets()`, `._write_reset()`, `.close()`.
- `horizon` = steps per episode; default = the full episode (`episode_length_s / dt` = 1000) and it is the `max_episode_length` baked into `build_advance`.
- `end_reasons` buckets every row at the end of the generation with the exact keys `END_REASONS = {"unfinished": 0, "timeout": 1, "tilted": 2, "error": 3}`: `error` is the genesis `scene.get_error_envs_mask()` flag, `tilted` is a roll/pitch beyond `termination_if_*_greater_than` in the `quat_to_xyz` convention, `timeout` is any other `done`, and `unfinished` means the row was still alive when the horizon or the early stop ended the generation.
- `stop_when_done=True` (default) ends the generation as soon as every row's `alive` is False; `stop_when_done=False` always simulates the full horizon (needed for throughput measurements and to exercise auto-reset/timeout).
- `go2_neat_train.py` CLI: `--pop-size` (8), `--generations` (2; 1 under pytest), `--horizon` (full 1000; 20 under pytest), `--episode-length-s` (20.0), `--master-seed` (0), `--perturb-base-pos` / `--perturb-base-rpy` / `--perturb-joint-pos` (0.05 each), `--no-perturbation`, `--stop-when-done` / `--no-stop-when-done`, `-e/--exp-name`, `-o/--output-dir` (`out/go2_neat`), `-g/--gpu`, `--evoxc-backend` and `--evoxc-device` (rollout), `--tell-backend {numpy,iree,xla}` (inherits the rollout backend, except on ROCm where it defaults to `xla`), `--pjrt-plugin` (PJRT plugin `.so` for the `xla` backend; also exported as `EVOXC_PJRT_PLUGIN` when that variable is unset), `--residency {numpy,device}` (`numpy`), `-v/--vis`, `--quiet`. Env vars: `GS_HSA_REEXEC=0` disables the `gs.init` ROCm HSA-runtime repair (see "AMD Instinct MI300X VF / ROCm (measured)").
- Trainer loop (owned by `trainer.NeuroevolutionTrainer`): `DefaultGenome(num_inputs=45, num_outputs=12, max_nodes=80, max_conns=640)` -> `NEAT(gen, pop_size=NP, species_size=3)` -> `Pipeline(algo, TraceOnlyProblem(45, 12), seed=evolution_seed(master_seed), backend="numpy", is_detailed=False)` -> `state = pipe.setup()` -> `config = pipe._static_config(state)`; then per generation ask (`pop` leaves) -> `env.evaluate` -> fitness aggregation -> `run_tell`.
- `TraceOnlyProblem(num_inputs, num_outputs)` (the shared `trainer.TraceOnlyProblem`) only declares `input_shape=(45,)` / `output_shape=(12,)`; its `evaluate` raises `NotImplementedError` because all real evaluation happens in the host loop.
- `go2_neat_train.py` is CLI + wiring + artifacts only: it builds `Go2NeatEnv` (passing `seed_table=generation_seed_table(master_seed, generations)`), constructs `NeuroevolutionTrainer(env, pop_size, generations, master_seed, num_inputs=NUM_OBS, num_outputs=NUM_ACTIONS, max_nodes=MAX_NODES, max_conns=MAX_CONNS, species_size=SPECIES_SIZE, tell_backend=args.tell_backend, plugin_path=args.pjrt_plugin, output_dir=output_dir, seed_table=seed_table)`, then calls `trainer.setup()` and `trainer.run()` and relies on `trainer.tell_backend` / `trainer.tell_device` / `trainer.tell_device_name` for the header and the teardown.
- The same `seed_table` array goes to the env and to the trainer, so the episodes the env runs are provably the rows the trainer records.
- The script prints two blocks to stdout (both suppressed by `--quiet`): the header (`pop_size`/`rows`/`generations`/`horizon`/`max_episode_length`/genesis/`evoxc` backend+device/tell backend+device/`master_seed`/`evolution_seed`/perturbation) and the final `done: ...` line; the per-generation `gen ... | seeds ... | best ... | cache +N/+0 | ends ...` line comes from the trainer's INFO logger.
- All five graphs (obs, policy, advance, fitness, tell) are built once through `ExecutableCache` (the four env graphs in `Go2NeatEnv.__init__`, the tell graph in `trainer.setup()`); `setup()` logs `Setup done in ... s (tell ... s, cache 4 hits / 5 misses)` and every generation then logs `cache +1/+0` (the tell lookup), i.e. zero rebuilds.
- Outputs: one compact log line per generation, `history.jsonl` (one JSON object per generation, written by the trainer) and `summary.json` (the trainer's config + records + totals, rewritten by the script with the Go2 run keys `horizon`, `episode_length_s`, `perturbation` {base_pos, base_rpy, joint_pos, is_zero}, `genesis_backend`, `evoxc_backend`, `evoxc_device`, `show_viewer`, `stop_when_done` merged into `config`) under `--output-dir`; both files are git-ignored run artifacts.
- `go2_train.py` stays the untouched PPO control path (rsl-rl); neither new module imports it.

### Device-resident rollout (`go2_device_rollout.py`)
- Public entry: `evaluate_device(env, *, executables, pop_arrays, seeds, init_qpos, row_init_qpos, horizon, stop_when_done)`; `Go2NeatEnv.evaluate` imports it lazily and dispatches when `env.residency == "device"`, so the numpy path never imports an IREE runtime.
- The device executables tuple is 7: `(obs, policy, advance, fitness, control, state, episode_returns)`; the last three are the graphs of `go2_device_kernels.py` and are compiled through `env.cache` keys `("control", rows, action_scale, clip)` / `("state", rows, 12, mask_is_bool)` / `("episode_returns", pop_size, 3)`.
- `DeviceArray.reshape` and every numpy function applied to a device array return a HOST `numpy.ndarray` (numpy dispatch), so a device buffer's shape is only changed by a graph: `episode_returns` is `go2_device_kernels.run_episode_returns` (a `evoxc.reshape`), never `DeviceArray.reshape`.
- The loop mirrors `_evaluate_numpy` step for step: policy -> write the PREVIOUS step's targets (`go2_env`'s `simulate_action_latency`) -> `scene.step()` -> state graph -> advance graph -> control graph (which produces the next step's targets and zeroes the rows that just ended) -> reset/`clear_errno`.
- The state graph is built for the error mask its scene hands out (`mask_is_bool`): a bool tensor with Torch, the zero-copy int32 view without it; one compiled graph cannot accept both dtypes.
- `_reset_rows` adapts the two `set_qpos` contracts: the torch-free staged setter takes the whole `(rows, 19)` tile plus `envs_idx`, the Torch setter scatters the selected rows.
- `--torch-free` (in `go2_neat_train.py`) is single-backend: it implies `--residency device`, forwards `tell_backend=None`/`plugin_path=None` so evolution runs on the env's own `iree@rocm:0`, rejects `--tell-backend xla`/`--pjrt-plugin`, and raises as soon as `torch` reaches the process.
- The whole generation runs with device-resident `evoxc` operands: observations, raw/clipped actions, `last_actions`, `returns`, `alive`, `episode_length`, the PD targets and the per-generation init-qpos tile.
- torch -> IREE: `evoxc.core.Tensor(IreeDevicePayload(rt.DeviceArray(hal, view), executable.device))` where `hal.from_dlpack_capsule(tensor.__dlpack__())` wraps the same memory (the call form of `evoxc.backends.adapters.iree._device_array_from_dlpack`); the tensor must be contiguous and on the executable's device.
- IREE -> torch: `torch.from_dlpack(evoxc_tensor)` aliases the IREE buffer (used only for the raw action); graph output -> graph input (obs to the policy graph, `returns`/`alive`/`episode_length` back into the advance graph) is passed through with no staging at all.
- State is read from live solver buffers, not the cloning getters: `solver.rigid_info.qpos` (`(19, rows)`, batch last, `[x,y,z,qw,qx,qy,qz, dof0..dof11]`) and `solver.dyn_state.dofs.pos/.vel` via `qd_to_torch(..., transpose=True, copy=False)`, cached per generation; base velocity/angular velocity keep `robot.get_vel()`/`get_ang()` because they compose `cd_vel + cd_ang x (pos - root_COM)` on the device.
- Syncs: `torch.cuda.synchronize()` (torch maps HIP) twice per step (after the state read, after the policy graph) and once per generation after the reset writes; `IreeExecutable.run` blocks on its own queue, so exported outputs are complete when handed on.
- Host touches per step: the `done` mask (`rows` bytes, for `np.flatnonzero(done)`, `end_reasons` and the host `alive` mirror that `stop_when_done` checks) and `error_mask.any()` (one byte, gating `scene.clear_errno()`); on an ending step only, `base_rpy_deg` and the `error_mask` rows for `_classify_endings`. Per generation: `returns`, `fitness`, the four pop leaves, and the `(rows,19)` init-qpos tile (reset rows are then gathered with a device `index_select`, so a reset costs no host -> device copy).
- bool / int32 leaves (`alive`, `done`, `episode_length`, `error_mask`) DLPack-export and import cleanly on this host, so no graph dtype override and no `payload.to_host()` fallback exists.
- The module never calls `env.executables()` (the four executables are passed in), which is why the cache counters are identical between residencies.

### Seed rules
- `np.random.SeedSequence(master_seed).spawn(2)` gives `(evolution_seq, env_seq)`; `evolution_seed(master_seed)` is the first child (`evolution_seed(0) = 3757552657`) and feeds `Pipeline(seed=...)`, while the second child drives `generation_seed_table` — the evolution and environment streams are independent.
- The seed table holds one row per generation with 3 distinct seeds per row; a generation's ordered triple is shared by every policy (and by all three rows of a policy), so all policies are compared on identical reset perturbations.
- `perturbation_from_seed` draws the whole reset (base position offset, a small w-first `base_quat` rotation, 12 joint offsets, each component uniform in `[-p, p]`) from `default_rng(seed)` alone and returns exact zeros when `Perturbation.is_zero`, so the zero-perturbation config reproduces the original fixed reset bit-for-bit.
- `perturbed_init_qpos` composes the sampled rotation as `q_pert * q_init` (`transform_quat_by_quat(init_quat, drawn["base_quat"])` = `quatmul(pert, init)` = `R_pert @ R_init`), i.e. the sampled `base_rpy` rotation acts in world axes after the authored init orientation; the opposite order (`R_init @ R_pert`, body-axis) is a real behavioural difference for a non-identity init quaternion.
- The Go2 config's `CFG["base_init_quat"]` is `[1,0,0,0]`, so both composition orders agree bit-for-bit and no measured number below depends on the order.
- A perturbed episode keeps its perturbation across auto-reset (post-reset episodes are discarded by the `alive`/`returns` masking), so a fresh seed per episode is unnecessary.
- Fitness = mean of the policy's 3 episode returns, where an episode return includes the step that ended it (first done inclusive).
- Same `master_seed` + same flags implies a byte-identical seed table, per-generation seeds, returns and fitness arrays.

### RL stack
- `go2_env.py` → `Go2Env` class: vectorized (`num_envs`) Go2 environment using `tensordict.TensorDict` for observations.
  Control runs at 50 Hz (`dt=0.02`, `substeps=2`), driving 12 motors via PD position control (`control_dofs_position`).
  `step(actions)` returns `(obs TensorDict, rew_buf, reset_buf, extras)`; `reset()` returns the initial `TensorDict`.
  Observation is a single `"policy"` tensor (45 dims): 3 base ang-vel + 3 projected gravity + 3 commands + 12 dof-pos + 12 dof-vel + 12 actions.
  Reward functions are auto-discovered from `reward_cfg["reward_scales"]` keys via `_reward_<name>` methods.
- `go2_train.py` → PPO training entry point via `rsl_rl.runners.OnPolicyRunner`; default 4096 parallel envs on GPU, logs to `logs/<exp_name>/` (pickles `cfgs.pkl`, saves `model_<iter>.pt`).
- `go2_eval.py` → loads a trained checkpoint (`logs/<exp_name>/model_<ckpt>.pt`) and runs the inference policy in a single viewer env on CPU; clears reward scales before building the env.
- `go2_backflip.py` → defines `BackflipEnv(Go2Env)` (60-dim phase-augmented observation, no commands/rewards) and plays `single.pt`/`double.pt` checkpoints from `backflip/`.

## Run commands
Run from the repo root; the script's own directory lands on `sys.path`, so `go2_neat_env` / `go2_neat_kernels` import by bare module name.
- Smoke, short horizon, NP=8 (the fastest end-to-end check, 2 generations): `python examples\locomotion\go2_neat_train.py --pop-size 8 --generations 2 --horizon 25 --output-dir out\neat_smoke` → best fitness −7.3785 / −7.3180, 21 then 10 steps.
- Full episode length, NP=8 (3 generations, no early stop): `python examples\locomotion\go2_neat_train.py --pop-size 8 --generations 3 --no-stop-when-done --output-dir out\neat_np8_full` → 3000 steps, ≈17 s.
- Full episode length, NP=16 (48 rows): `python examples\locomotion\go2_neat_train.py --pop-size 16 --generations 3 --no-stop-when-done --output-dir out\neat_np16_full` → 3000 steps, ≈27 s.
- NP=32 has NOT been measured; expect ≈4× the NP=8 row count and re-check `max_collision_pairs=20`.
- Zero-perturbation parity run: `python examples\locomotion\go2_neat_train.py --pop-size 8 --generations 2 --horizon 25 --no-perturbation --output-dir out\neat_noperturb` → best fitness −7.4132 / −7.4132.
- Device residency (opt-in, needs a non-CPU `evoxc` device): add `--residency device` to any of the commands above, e.g. `python examples/locomotion/go2_neat_train.py -g --evoxc-backend iree --evoxc-device rocm:0 --tell-backend numpy --residency device --pop-size 8 --generations 2 --horizon 25 --output-dir out/neat_device`; pairwise with the same command without `--residency device` `seeds` / `fitness` / `steps` / `end_reasons` / `init_qpos` are bit-identical and `returns` agrees to ≤4.8e-07 (see "Residency parity").
- `--residency device` on a cpu-kind device is rejected up front with a `ValueError` naming the supported configuration; it never silently falls back to host staging.
- Reproduction check: run the smoke command twice with the same `--master-seed 0` into two `--output-dir`s and compare `history.jsonl` (seed table + fitness) — both runs must be identical.

## Simulation boundary and data paths
This section covers `go2_neat_env.py` under the default `residency="numpy"`; `go2_device_rollout.py`'s zero-copy boundary is described under "Device-resident rollout", and `try1.py`'s own boundary under "Parity (measured)".
- Genesis owns the physics; the compiled graphs own observation / policy / reward / termination; `evaluate()` is the only loop that alternates between them.
- `read_state()` is the single device -> host boundary: `robot.get_pos()`, `get_quat()`, `get_vel()`, `get_ang()`, `get_dofs_position(motors_dof_idx)`, `get_dofs_velocity(motors_dof_idx)` and `scene.get_error_envs_mask()`, each converted with `genesis.utils.misc.tensor_to_array(...).astype(np.float32)` (bool for the mask); the module imports no torch.
- The reason for that conversion is that the genesis state lives in torch tensors on `gs.device` while `run_obs` / `run_policy` / `run_advance` / `run_fitness` are numpy-facing and stage their own operands (`evoxc.from_numpy` when the executable is not on CPU), so observations, actions and returns never leave the host.
- `go2_neat_env.py` imports no torch: the only torch left on the numpy path is Genesis's own (`gs.init` imports it, and every `robot.get_*` accessor returns a torch tensor on `gs.device` that `tensor_to_array` turns into a host array). `go2_device_rollout.py` keeps its torch usage and owns its own device-side `_inv_init_quat` and `actions_dof_idx` tensor instead of reading the env's now-NumPy attributes.
- `base_quat_rel` is computed on the host with `_quat_mul(robot.get_quat() host copy, self._inv_init_quat)`, a NumPy transcription of `genesis.utils.geom._tc_quat_mul`'s coefficient form (`_QUAT_MUL_COEFFICIENTS` must stay identical to `_tc_geom_constants().quat_mul`, which is what makes it faithful), because the numba `transform_quat_by_quat` product drifts from the torch reference (2.1e-4 on a policy fitness over 21 steps); `quat_to_xyz(..., rpy=True, degrees=True)` then uses its NumPy branch.
- `_write_targets(targets)` is one host -> device write: the NumPy `(rows,12)` array straight into `robot.control_dofs_position(...)` for the 12 motor slots (the setter takes `array_like`).
- `_write_reset(qpos, envs_idx)` is the other host -> device write: the `(rows,19)` NumPy qpos plus an optional NumPy `envs_idx`, into `robot.set_qpos(..., zero_velocity=True, skip_forward=True)`; `_reset_generation` keeps the generation's `init_quat` / `inv_init_quat` as host NumPy arrays (`np.ascontiguousarray(row_init_qpos[:, 3:7], np.float32)` then the NumPy `inv_quat`).
- `motors_dof_idx` (int32) / `actions_dof_idx` (`np.argsort` of it) / `_control_dof_slice` are built once in `__init__`; the index arrays are NumPy and go straight to the setters, while `_motors_dof_idx_np` / `_qpos_dof_slot` index host arrays.
- The observation of a fresh reset needs no simulation step: it is computed by `run_obs` from the known reset values (zero base angular velocity, identity orientation relative to the init pose, the init joint positions, zero joint velocities and actions), exactly like `go2_env.reset()`.
- Handover cost is not instrumented separately for the neat env; at NP=8 the whole generation runs 172–176 steps/s for 24 rows (≈5.7–5.8 ms per `scene.step()` plus the graph calls), so no boundary measurement is claimed beyond that.
- The device backends do not remove that staging: `run_obs` / `run_policy` / `run_advance` / `run_fitness` copy every input to the device (`evoxc.from_numpy(...).to(device)`) and materialize every output back on the host, so each step still performs a handful of small host<->device transfers (state, observation, actions, targets) of at most a few KB.
- With 24 rows the GPU rollout is therefore not faster than CPU (132.7–146.9 steps/s on MI300X/`iree@rocm:0` vs 172.1–176.0 steps/s on the CPU/numpy baseline); the GPU pays off in `tell()`, which cannot run on IREE/ROCm at all.

## Torch boundary (the migration contract)
The numpy residency of `go2_neat_env.py` / `go2_neat_train.py` contains no `import torch` and no torch operation of its own; `import genesis` still pulls torch into the process, so the stack is torch-free by our code, not by the process.
AST-scan proof (no `import torch` / `from torch ...`): `python -c "import ast,sys;[print(p,[n.names[0].name for n in ast.walk(ast.parse(open(p).read())) if isinstance(n,ast.Import) and 'torch' in n.names[0].name]) for p in sys.argv[1:]]" examples/locomotion/go2_neat_env.py examples/locomotion/go2_neat_train.py`.

| source of torch | why it is there | what would remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `import genesis` (`gs.init`, `gs.Scene.build`, solvers) | Genesis is torch/quadrants-based and imports torch at package import | nothing on this side; only a torch-free Genesis/quadrants build |
| `robot.get_pos/get_quat/get_vel/get_ang/get_dofs_position/get_dofs_velocity`, `scene.get_error_envs_mask` | every accessor returns a `torch.Tensor` on `gs.device`; `genesis.utils.misc.tensor_to_array` is the one call that turns it into a host array | array-returning accessors in `genesis/` |
| `robot.control_dofs_position` / `set_qpos` / `set_dofs_kp` / `set_dofs_kv` | the setters take `array_like` and convert NumPy to torch internally (`indices_to_mask` / `assign_indexed_tensor`) | a torch-free setter path in `genesis/` |
| `go2_device_rollout.py` | the zero-copy DLPack bridge needs torch (`qd_to_torch`, `torch.from_dlpack`); imported lazily, so the numpy path never loads it | by design; a quadrants-side capsule producer and a Genesis setter accepting a foreign device pointer |
| `tensorneat` / `evoxc` (via `go2_neat_kernels` and the `../neuroevolution` layer) | their own packages; the compiled graphs themselves are numpy/StableHLO | nothing here — they are the graph runtime |

- The relative base quaternion is the one quantity this boundary has to compute itself, and it is the only place where "torch-free" costs arithmetic: `_quat_mul` (NumPy, coefficient form) can differ from `transform_quat_by_quat` on the torch path by 1 ulp per component, and the graphs' float32 rounding absorbs it.
- Measured migration parity (this host, CPU/numpy, pop 8, 2 x horizon 25): `seeds`, `steps` (21 then 10), `end_reasons` (tilted=24 both), `init_qpos`, `returns`, `episode_returns` and `fitness` are all bit-identical to the pre-migration torch boundary (max abs diff 0.0), and the full-horizon 3 x 1000 run reproduces best fitness -7.3785 / -7.3180 / -6.0399 with tilted=24 in every generation.
- The two residencies now differ by that same sub-ULP effect (device path still uses the torch product on the GPU): 4.77e-07 on `returns` in generation 0, everything else bit-identical — see "Residency parity".

## Constraints
- Scripts are plain modules, not a package; run from the repo root, e.g. `python examples/locomotion/try1.py --headless` or `python examples/locomotion/go2_neat_train.py ...`.
- `try1.py` imports `go2_evox_kernels` by bare module name (the script directory is on `sys.path`); both need `evoxc` installed, which is NOT declared in `pyproject.toml`.
- Control/reward math lives exclusively in `go2_evox_kernels.py`; `try1.py` must not re-derive the standing pose, `dt`, or the reward terms.
- `go2_neat_kernels.py` is deliberately one module (the Go2 task graphs and constants, ~660 lines): it is the single import surface the env/train/device modules build against and it owns the stack's only `sys.path` shim into `../neuroevolution`, so do not split it. Its re-exports (`build_policy` adapter, `build_fitness`/`run_fitness`/`build_tell`/`run_tell`/`ExecutableCache`, `EPISODES_PER_POLICY`/`Perturbation`; plus `perturbation_from_seed` in `go2_neat_env.py`) exist so the graph/seeding tests and `../drone` keep importing unchanged: each is a one-line delegation and none may grow logic.
- The compiled graphs are shared by both residencies, so neither the graph dtypes/signatures nor the compute may be specialized for DLPack; bool/int32 leaves cross the DLPack bridge as `torch.bool`/`torch.int32` on this host.
- Device residency needs a non-CPU `evoxc` device backed by IREE (`iree` on `rocm:0`/`cuda:0`); a `numpy`/`cpu:0` configuration is rejected at construction and never silently falls back to host staging. The numpy path never imports an IREE runtime (the device module imports it lazily).
- `go2_train.py` / `go2_eval.py` enforce `rsl-rl-lib>=5.0.0` via `importlib.metadata` at import time.
- `go2_env.py` imports `tensordict.TensorDict` and helpers from `genesis.utils.geom` (quat transforms).
- `go2_reward.png` is a git-ignored run artifact next to the script, never tracked.

## Design Decisions
- Static graph specializations (standing pose, phases, dt, 1/action_scale, thresholds, max_episode_length, tiled commands) keep the runtime signature to genuinely per-step values only, and the `run_*` wrappers re-pass them on every call.
- The `run_*` wrappers do input staging (`evoxc.from_numpy` when the executable is not on CPU) and output materialization, so the demo only performs boundary conversion.
- `SimOptions(dt=0.02, substeps=2)` matches the real robot's 50 Hz control rate.
- `simulate_action_latency=True` executes the previous step's actions (`last_actions`) to mirror the real robot's 1-step latency; the graphs see the raw action of step `t` and clip it themselves.
- `RigidOptions(enable_self_collision=False, max_collision_pairs=20, tolerance=1e-5)` is tuned for a locomotion policy with few contact pairs (saves memory).
- One scene with `3 * pop_size` rows (rather than 3 sequential episodes per policy) keeps every policy on identical reset perturbations and amortizes one `scene.step()` across all rows.
- Backflip policies use a phase clock (`sin`/`cos` of `episode_length_buf`) in the observation instead of velocity commands.
- `tell()` backend selection is device-gated: ROCm defaults to `xla` (IREE cannot compile that graph there), CUDA keeps `iree` plus `--iree-codegen-llvmgpu-use-reduction-vector-distribution=false`, CPU keeps `numpy`, and an explicit `--tell-backend` always wins.
- The PJRT plugin is passed explicitly instead of being discovered: `--pjrt-plugin` forwards a `plugin_path` compile option to `build_tell` and fills `EVOXC_PJRT_PLUGIN` only when that variable is unset, so the plugin choice is visible on the command line and nothing global changes for the CUDA/CPU paths.
- ROCm-specific workarounds stay device-gated: the `xla`-on-ROCm `tell()` default, the `plugin_path` option and the teardown `os._exit(0)` (see Gotchas) only take effect when the resolved `tell()` device is a non-CPU ROCm device.
- The HSA-runtime repair is owned by `gs.init` (`genesis/utils/hsa.py`), not by this stack: it re-executes the process with the system `libhsa-runtime64` preloaded because `LD_PRELOAD` must exist before the dynamic loader maps torch's HIP/HSA libraries and a `ctypes` preload is provably insufficient (see the MI300X section). It runs for a GPU/ROCm backend only, so CPU/numpy runs never pay for it; `GS_HSA_REEXEC=0` opts out and `GS_HSA_REEXEC_DONE=1` is its sentinel. The script and `Go2NeatEnv` add nothing to it.

- `jax-rocm7-pjrt` 0.10.2 (PJRT C-API plugin, not declared in `pyproject.toml`) is what makes the `xla` backend usable on ROCm; pass it with `--pjrt-plugin <plugin.so>` or install the wheel and let evoxc's `jax_plugins/*/*.so` discovery find it.

- `tensorneat` 0.2.0 (editable from the local `evoxc` branch clone, not declared in `pyproject.toml`).
- `tensordict` (installed via the `dev` extra of `pyproject.toml`).
- `rsl-rl-lib>=5.0.0` (installed separately; not declared in `pyproject.toml`).
- `torch` and `genesis` (project core).
- The system ROCm HSA runtime (`/opt/rocm/lib/libhsa-runtime64.so.1.*`, 1.18.70100 with ROCm 7.1.0 here) is what long ROCm GPU rollouts need; torch 2.13.0+rocm7.1 bundles its own `torch/lib/libhsa-runtime64.so` (1.18.70101) that corrupts AQL packets on this host.
  It is an environment fact rather than a declared dependency: `go2_neat_train.py` selects the system runtime itself (see Design Decisions).

## Gotchas
- Pass a POSITIVE `axis` to `evoxc.concatenate`: evoxc copies a negative axis verbatim into StableHLO (`dimension = -1 : i64`) and `iree-compile` rejects it as a non-negative-integer attribute violation; `evoxc.stack(..., axis=-1)` is safe because it emits `stablehlo.reshape` + a positive dimension.
- Under pytest (`PYTEST_VERSION` in the environment) `try1.py` creates no viewer and runs 5 steps, mirroring the shortened `go2_backflip.py` playback, so the example smoke test cannot hang on the viewer loop; explicit `--headless` (250 steps) and `--max-steps N` still win over that cap.
- `scene.viewer` is `None` whenever the viewer is not shown, so `try1.py` only touches `scene.viewer` (follow / `is_alive`) when it actually created one.
- The gait `step` input must be a 0-d `int32` ndarray; `np.ascontiguousarray` promotes 0-d to shape `(1,)` and the executable rejects it.
- Static ndarray leaves specialize the graph but must still be passed to `evoxc.run` on every call (validated with `array_equal`).
- Graph-time constants need `evoxc.constant(evoxc.from_numpy(arr))`; `evoxc.zeros` is a concrete creator, not a graph op.
- `scene.clear_errno()` must be called for every row the graphs report in `error_mask` (and once per generation reset): a latched solver error otherwise re-raises `GenesisException: Invalid constraint forces causing 'nan'` on the next `scene.step()` although the graph already marked those rows dead.
- The trainer's per-generation `end_reasons` count comes from the graphs' `done`/`alive` state plus the genesis error mask; `tilted` dominates for untrained policies at the full horizon.
- `max_collision_pairs=20`: exceeding ~20 collision pairs raises an exception; keep policy contact sparse.
- `base_init_quat` differs between walking (`[1,0,0,0]`) and backflip (`[0,0,0,1]`) configs; the backflip robot starts inverted.
- `run_advance` takes `base_quat` already relative to the init pose (identity for the Go2 config) and world-frame `get_vel()`/`get_ang()`; the graph rotates by `inv(base_quat)`.
- In `run_advance` the timeout fires when `episode_length + 1 > max_episode_length`; the dying step's reward is counted exactly once, `alive` never re-arms, and `episode_length` restarts at 0 on that step.
- Roll/pitch termination is `|sinp| > tanθ·cosp` and `|sinrcosp| > tanθ·cosrcosp`, with the `cosrcosp·cosp <= 0` quadrant guard; do not add a second `cosp` factor (that shrinks the roll threshold with pitch and fires false positives).
- `build_policy` logs an `evoxc` `UserWarning` about embedding the pop batch as a >1 MiB graph constant (raise `EVOXC_LARGE_CONSTANT_BYTES` if `pop_size` grows).
- `--stop-when-done` (the default) truncates a full-horizon run as soon as every row has tilted, so quote throughput from `--no-stop-when-done` runs; short-horizon generation 0 is warm-up dominated either way.
- Backflip `.pt` checkpoints are data (torch `state_dict`s), not code; they are loaded with `torch.load(..., weights_only=True)`.
- `go2_train.py` expects a GPU for the 4096-env throughput; `go2_eval.py`/`go2_backflip.py`/`try1.py`/`go2_neat_train.py` run on CPU or GPU.
- A mixed IREE-rollout + XLA-`tell()` process must re-select the executable's own backend before staging: `evoxc.backends.get(name)` sets a per-device-kind "preferred transfer backend" (last lookup wins), so `_to_device_array` calls `evoxc.backends.get(executable.backend)` before `evoxc.from_numpy(...).to(device)`; otherwise IREE rollout operands are staged by the XLA provider and fail with "the xla adapter requires a PJRT C API plugin" (or `DeviceError` when both are on `rocm:0`).
- Pass `evoxc.Device("rocm", 0)` (or go through `resolve_backend_device`): the string `"rocm:0"` handed straight to `evoxc.build` is mis-parsed as `Device(kind="rocm:0")`.
- On ROCm the XLA client reserves a 143.77 GiB BFC pool plus up to 51.5 GB in its Collective allocator at creation time, so it needs an otherwise idle GPU; it also warns `TF_XLA_HSACO_CACHE_DIR is not set`, meaning kernels are re-compiled (and cached only in memory) on every process start.
- The XLA/ROCm client can abort during interpreter teardown (HIP/HSA shutdown), so `go2_neat_train.py` flushes stdout/stderr and then `os._exit(0)` once a device-resident XLA `tell()` run has finished; this only applies when `--tell-backend xla` targets a non-CPU device.
- On ROCm the tell graph never finishes `iree-compile` (the process stays `R` indefinitely), so never wait on `--tell-backend iree` there; the trainer defaults ROCm `tell()` to `xla` for that reason.
- Any Genesis GPU run, `Go2NeatEnv`/`try1.py` included, gets the HSA repair from `gs.init`; only a process that skips `gs.init` (or sets `GS_HSA_REEXEC=0`) keeps torch's bundled runtime and aborts a long rollout with `HSA_STATUS_ERROR_INVALID_PACKET_FORMAT` after ~50-600 steps, so such a run must set `LD_PRELOAD=<system libhsa-runtime64.so.1.*>` itself.
- When preloading, preload only the system `libhsa-runtime64`: preloading the system `libamdhip64` too maps two HIP runtimes into one process and SIGSEGVs before the scene builds.
- The re-exec sentinel is `GS_HSA_REEXEC_DONE=1` (set by `gs.init` for the child); `GS_HSA_REEXEC=0` is the user-facing opt-out and also the control for reproducing the abort. The notice is emitted at INFO level, so the trainer's `logging_level="warning"` hides it.
- `qd_to_torch(field, row_mask, col_mask, transpose=True, copy=False)`: for a 2-D `(n, envs)` field the batch axis is the column, so a joint/dof selection must go in the SECOND argument; passing a slice as the row mask silently selects the batch axis and yields a transposed view that later fails to broadcast as a graph operand.
- A DLPack export must be compact: `qd_to_torch(copy=False)` views of the solver buffers are non-contiguous, so the device path materializes `.contiguous()` / `index_select` slices before importing them into IREE (device copies, not host traffic).
- `--residency device` never calls `Go2NeatEnv.executables()`; it receives the four executables and reads the solver buffers directly, and any future residency must keep the same rule or the cache counters stop matching the numpy path.

## Test Strategy
- `tests/test_examples.py` smoke-tests `try1.py` as a subprocess: no viewer, 5 steps under `PYTEST_VERSION`, and skipped where `evoxc` is missing via the `EXAMPLE_DEPENDENCIES` entry for `try1.py`.
- No test asserts the torch-free contract or the `_quat_mul` faithfulness yet: the AST-scan one-liner in "Torch boundary (the migration contract)" and an equality check of `_QUAT_MUL_COEFFICIENTS` / `_quat_mul` against `genesis.utils.geom._tc_geom_constants().quat_mul` belong in the tests node (sibling — report, never edit here).
- That harness ignores `*_train.py`, `*_eval.py`, `*_env.py`, `behavior_cloning.py`, `quadcopter_controller.py`, so `go2_neat_train.py` / `go2_neat_env.py` are documentation, not smoke tests; `go2_backflip.py` is the other smoke test here and shortens playback to 5 steps when `PYTEST_VERSION` is set.
- `go2_evox_kernels.py`, `go2_legacy_reference.py`, `go2_neat_kernels.py` and `go2_device_rollout.py` are shared helper modules, not runnable demos, and must be in `tests/test_examples.py`'s `IGNORE_SCRIPT_PATTERNS`; `go2_device_rollout.py` is NOT matched by the `*_train.py`/`*_env.py`/`*_eval.py` patterns, so until the tests node adds it (and an `EXAMPLE_DEPENDENCIES` entry, if it must stay runnable) the example harness would try to execute it. Files under `tests/` are a sibling node: report such changes, never edit them here.
- The three graph/seeding test files still import the moved symbols through the `go2_neat_kernels` re-exports and the `go2_neat_env.perturbation_from_seed` adapter, so they pass unchanged; the follow-up (owned by the tests node) is to point `tests/test_go2_neat_seeding.py` at `seeding`/`env_base` directly and `tests/test_go2_neat_kernels.py` at `evolution_graphs`/`policy_graphs`, and to pass `num_inputs`/`num_outputs` to `build_policy` instead of relying on the adapter.
- `tests/test_go2_evox_parity.py` (`@pytest.mark.required`, `importorskip("evoxc")`) covers the graph math without a simulator: 250 chained gait steps (5e-5 tolerance), exact gait semantics at steps 0/25/26, and 200 randomized states (1e-5 per reward term, 1e-6 on the total) against the `go2_legacy_reference.py` oracle.
- `tests/test_go2_neat_seeding.py` covers the seeding/perturbation stack: seed-table shape, reproducibility and prefix stability, evolution-stream independence, perturbation draw determinism/bounds/order, the drawn-quaternion-vs-rpy agreement, zero-perturbation bit-identity, row layout, and that `perturbed_init_qpos` composes the rotation AFTER the init orientation (`R_pert @ R_init`).
- `tests/test_go2_neat_kernels.py` (`@pytest.mark.required`, `importorskip("evoxc")` + `importorskip("tensorneat")`) covers the compiled-graph layer without a simulator: the policy-major row mapping (`3p..3p+2` with the genome payload consumed in the same order), first-done/error/timeout truncation with `alive` never re-arming, and `run_fitness` as the maximized mean of a policy's 3 episode returns.
- The same module checks the numeric contracts: observation parity to ~1e-6 against an independent numpy `go2_env._update_observation`, reward parity to ~1e-5 per term and ~1e-9 on the total against `go2_legacy_reference.walking_reward_terms`, roll/pitch termination agreement with `genesis.utils.geom.quat_to_xyz(..., rpy=True, degrees=True)` over thousands of random, near-threshold and |pitch|≈90° quaternions, and `ExecutableCache` hit/miss/clear behavior.
